- **Format:** In-person conversation, about 30 minutes long
- **Editing:** The entire conversation was transcribed and recorded using Apple's Voice Memo app. Light editing was done afterwards to fix transcription mistakes and keep the transcript readable by keeping Lucas' one-word replies ("Yeah," "Yep," "Gotcha") inside P3's turns as *[Lucas: Yeah.]*. Personally identifying information and information not relevant to the interview were also removed.
- **Recruitment:** A neighbour of the interviewer, recruited directly
- **Consent:** P3 verbally consented to the interview and its recording prior to the recording beginning.
- **Anonymization:** P3 is not named. All identifying details have been removed.
