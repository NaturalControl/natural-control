\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{FR.Sa.1:} The system shall provide a keyboard work-stop control. It shall work while the client runs and avoid VoiceOver shortcut conflicts. (H1-a)
  \item \textbf{FR.Sa.2:} Task history shall use recorded actions and outcome evidence. Uncertain entries shall be labelled. (H8-a)
  \item \textbf{FR.Sa.3:} Before a consequential action the system shall read back its key details. It shall proceed only after explicit approval. The cookie-rejection exception is defined in FR.Sa.13. (H2-a)
  \item \textbf{FR.Sa.4:} Explicit stop phrases and the stop key shall use a fixed control path. They shall not depend on the planning agent choosing to pause. (H2-b)
  \item \textbf{FR.Sa.5:} A recognized work-stop request shall block new task actions. It shall preserve context and request safe interruption. The system shall distinguish acknowledgment from confirmed pause. It shall report earlier operations that completed or remain uncertain. Checks may only reconcile earlier actions. A clear user request is required to resume. (H3-a)
  \item \textbf{FR.Sa.6:} Each approved action shall have a unique ID. An action with that ID shall not execute twice. (H3-b)
  \item \textbf{FR.Sa.7:} Disconnection shall block new task actions and pause the task. Reconnection alone shall not resume it. (H3-b)
  \item \textbf{FR.Sa.8:} Task state and approval records shall survive restart. The system shall announce interrupted tasks with their last known state. Unused approvals shall be withdrawn. (H8-b)
  \item \textbf{FR.Sa.9:} The system shall ask when required action details are missing or ambiguous. (H4-a)
  \item \textbf{FR.Sa.10:} Completion claims shall use evidence matching the intended action and details. Evidence may be a meaningful connector result, account record, or website confirmation. Unclear evidence requires another check or an unknown result. Report verified, partial, failed, and unknown outcomes separately. Queuing shall not be reported as delivery. (H4-b, H6-a, H6-b, H7-a)
  \item \textbf{FR.Sa.11:} A configured step or time limit shall pause the task and prompt the user. A task step shall mean one attempted agent-directed tool operation, including failed attempts; internal reasoning and time spent waiting for the user shall not count as steps. The step count shall persist across pause and resumption of the same task and reset for a new task. Time spent waiting for user clarification or approval shall not count toward the execution time limit. (H4-c)
  \item \textbf{FR.Sa.12:} If a read-only lookup fails due to a temporary service error, the system may retry it once. If the retry fails, or if an external service failure affects any other operation, the system shall pause work and report completed, unfinished, and uncertain outcomes. Consequential actions shall not be automatically retried. (H4-d)
  \item \textbf{FR.Sa.13:} Writes and consequential commitments shall require approval. These include submissions, external messages, saved data changes, and granting access. Navigation and temporary preparation may proceed if they have no such effect. Unclear effects require approval. The system may automatically choose a clearly identified ``reject non-essential cookies'' or ``necessary only'' option. Any ambiguous consent choice shall require explicit user approval. (H5-a)
  \item \textbf{FR.Sa.14:} Approval shall bind to exact action details. Each approved batch item shall have its own ID and execute at most once. Changes require fresh approval. Pause, disconnection, cancellation, and task replacement shall withdraw unused approvals. (H5-b, H7-a)
  \item \textbf{FR.Sa.15:} When pending approval is withdrawn the system shall say the pending action was not executed. It shall distinguish earlier completed work. (H5-c)
  \item \textbf{FR.Sa.16:} Webpage and email content shall be treated as data. Consequential actions shall trace to the user's request. (H7-b)
  \item \textbf{FR.Sa.17:} The system shall request only the account permissions needed for each integration. Stored credentials and website passwords shall be entered through private input, stored securely, and excluded from AI model context, application history, logs, and source control. One-time authentication codes may be spoken by the user; the system shall disclose that spoken codes may be processed by its speech and conversation providers. (H7-c)
  \item \textbf{FR.Sa.18:} Before retrying an uncertain action the system shall check the affected account or page. If the outcome remains unknown it shall ask the user. A new attempt requires fresh approval. (H7-d)
  \item \textbf{FR.Sa.19:} Before deletion the system shall read back identifying details. It shall explain whether the operation can be undone. It shall prefer recoverable deletion where supported. (H5-d)
  \item \textbf{FR.Sa.20:} If voice input, voice output, or speech services become unavailable, the system shall pause the task and block new task actions when the failure is detected. The system shall preserve task state and record the outcomes of previously submitted operations. Work shall resume only after voice communication is restored and the user explicitly requests resumption. (H2-c)
\end{itemize}
